Privacy Policy

OctoBuddy values the privacy of its users. This section describes the intended approach to handling personal information when students use the platform.

1. Information We May Collect

Depending on the features provided, the platform may collect account details, contact information, tutoring bookings, academic support requests and relevant platform activity.

2. How Information May Be Used

Information may be used to manage student accounts, coordinate tutoring sessions, provide support, improve platform functionality and maintain platform security.

3. Sharing of Information

Personal information should only be shared where necessary for legitimate platform operations, with appropriate safeguards and a lawful basis. Information should not be sold or disclosed without proper authorisation or another valid legal basis.

4. Security

Appropriate technical and organisational safeguards should be implemented to protect personal information against unauthorised access, loss, misuse or disclosure.

5. Your Rights

Subject to applicable law, users may have rights to access, correct or request deletion of their personal information and to raise concerns about how it is used.

6. Contact About Privacy

For privacy-related questions or requests, contact the designated OctoBuddy support or privacy contact through the official channels provided by the platform.

Important: This is a draft policy template. It must be checked against OctoBuddy's actual data practices and applicable South African privacy requirements, including POPIA, before publication.

Terms & Conditions

By using OctoBuddy, users are expected to act responsibly, respect other students and comply with the platform's applicable rules.

1. Acceptable Use

Users must use OctoBuddy for legitimate academic support, learning and collaboration. The platform must not be used for harassment, fraud, harmful activities or unlawful purposes.

2. Student Responsibilities

Students should provide accurate account information, attend booked sessions where possible and communicate respectfully with other users.

3. OctoBuddy Responsibilities

Users offering tutoring or academic assistance should provide honest, respectful support and avoid misrepresenting their qualifications or abilities.

4. Academic Integrity

OctoBuddy should support learning and understanding. Users must not use the platform to facilitate plagiarism, cheating, impersonation or other forms of academic misconduct.

5. Accounts and Security

Users are responsible for protecting their login details and should report suspected unauthorised access through the appropriate support channels.

6. Platform Availability

Features may occasionally be unavailable because of maintenance, technical issues or updates. Continuous availability cannot be guaranteed.

7. Changes to These Terms

The platform's terms may be updated when its features, policies or legal requirements change. Users should review the current terms periodically.

8. Questions About These Terms

Contact the OctoBuddy support team through the official support channels if you need clarification about these terms.

Important: These terms are a starting template, not a final legal agreement. Review them against the platform's actual functionality and applicable laws before publishing.
