What OctoBuddy Offers

Peer-to-Peer Learning

Connect with knowledgeable Octo Buddies who have successfully completed the same modules you're studying. Learn from fellow students in a supportive environment where asking questions and gaining confidence is encouraged.

Easy Tutoring Booking

Finding academic support is simple. Book tutoring sessions through an easy-to-use scheduling system, view your upcoming sessions, and receive guidance whenever you need it most.

Learn, Teach & Earn Points

Take your learning a step further by becoming an Octo Buddy. Share your knowledge with other students, earn 25 points for every completed tutoring session, and build valuable leadership and mentoring experience while making a positive impact.
